Ongoing research projects

Investigating the IGM and CGM

The intergalactic and circumgalactic media shape how galaxies form and evolve. I want to understand how the Mg II absorbers seen in the spectra of background quasars are connected to galaxies.

Using mock quasar absorption spectra generated from cosmological simulations such as IllustrisTNG and SIMBA, I am asking whether these absorbers come from material blown out by star-forming galaxies or from large rotating disks accreting gas onto them.

Mg II absorbers IllustrisTNG SIMBA Mock spectra

O VI in the Intra-group Medium

I compare O VI absorption in synthetic quasar spectra of 14 galaxy groups each from TNG50 and SIMBA, with 90,000 sightlines per group, against the COS-IGrM survey. Both simulations fall short of the observed covering fraction: 20.6 ± 2.6% in TNG50 and 6.0 ± 0.8% in SIMBA, against 44 ± 5% in COS-IGrM. About 95% of the simulated absorbers are gravitationally bound, and in TNG50 the strongest sit within about 200 kpc of star-forming galaxies, which SIMBA does not show. I published this as a first-author paper in The Astrophysical Journal.

Spectral Classification of AGN and Supernovae

I am building a contingent Bayesian-logic (BLOG) model that classifies AGN and supernova spectra from emission-line detections, widths and ratios. The model includes dust attenuation and variability, and a line-fitting and identification pipeline I built informs its priors. I work on this with Prof. Siddharth Srivastava and Dr. Rolf Jansen.

Kinematics of the Outer Circumgalactic Medium

I am looking for co-rotating and counter-rotating gas in the outer circumgalactic medium of L* galaxies, using sightline-based synthetic spectroscopy in TNG50-1. I have produced about 75,000 synthetic spectra in Lyα, C II λ1334 and Si III λ1206, and I am now classifying their kinematics and working through the uncertainties.

Site quality analysis for an Indian submillimeter telescope

Raman Research Institute

I mapped precipitable water vapor (PWV) across Ladakh from 184 months of ERA5 reanalysis (2010–2025), after checking ERA5 against radiometer data at Hanle, Mauna Kea and Chajnantor. Two candidate regions reach PWV ≤ 1 mm for about 23% and 19% of that period, against about 5% and 8% for the Hanle and Merak grid cells. I began this as a visiting student with Prof. Mayuri S. Rao at the Raman Research Institute in 2022 and published it as a first-author paper in The Astronomical Journal in 2026.

Magnetic field topology in the 2017 eclipse corona

Senior thesis, IIT Delhi

I cleaned and aligned images of the 2017 total solar eclipse using phase correlation and unsharp masking, then retrieved the corona's closed and open magnetic field lines with the RHT and ACF methods. The aim was to check that topology against the MHD equations. I did this with Prof. Suprit Singh in 2022–2023.

Post-processing for entanglement-based QKD

SURA 2021, IIT Delhi

With Paras Sharma, I wrote Python software for the post-processing stage of entanglement-based quantum key distribution: several Cascade reconciliation variants, privacy amplification and networking. I verified it on experimental data from Prof. Bhaskar Kanseri’s lab at IIT Delhi. The project was selected for SURA 2021 from 27 proposals in the Physics and Electrical departments, and was recognised for exemplary results.

Identifying acoustic wave sources on the Sun

Solar data

I studied acoustic waves at the solar surface in simulated MURaM data, and located their sources with image-processing methods such as azimuthal averaging in OpenCV and NumPy.

Angle-dependent Fano profiles in cold atomic collisions

Atomic collisions

I studied how the asymmetry of Fano resonance profiles changes with scattering angle, using a complex-valued w parameter that gives an angle-dependent Fano q. For the orbiting resonance in cold H–Kr elastic scattering, computed with the VPA phase-shift program, the asymmetry varies strongly with angle and is sensitive to the interatomic potential. I did this with Prof. Fumihiro Koike and Prof. Yoshiro Azuma, and published it as a first-author paper in the Indian Journal of Physics in 2026.
